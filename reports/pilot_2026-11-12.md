# First real pilot: 3 papers, free-tier models (2026-11-12)

A small end-to-end run of the current (v2) pipeline: PDF -> chunks -> retrieval -> model -> saved results. It used the
small embedding model (all-MiniLM-L6-v2) and free API keys, so it says whether the pipeline works, not how accurate the
models are (there are no human labels yet).

| Model | Provider | Papers done | Answers | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| openai/gpt-oss-120b | Groq | 3 of 3 | 24 | 717 s total, median 32 s per answer |
| gemini-2.5-flash | Google | 1 of 3 | 8 | the other two papers hit the free-tier quota (429) |

## What the answers looked like (v2 prompt)

| | gpt-oss-120b | gemini-2.5-flash |
|---|---|---|
| Answers | 1: 12, 2: 2, 4: 3, 5: 4, Unknown: 3 | 1: 3, 2: 1, 4: 1, 5: 2, Unknown: 1 |
| Decision modes | full_context 13, retrieval 8, unresolved 3 | full_context 7, unresolved 1 |
| Confidence | min 0.42, median 0.82, max 0.95 | min 0.85, median 0.90, max 0.95 |
| Answers without cited evidence | 0 | 0 |

- The model is allowed to say `Unknown` (3 of 24 and 1 of 8). The old pipeline would have forced a 1-5 guess.
- gpt-oss-120b reported confidence as low as 0.42. The models in the original study never went below 0.6.
- Every answer cites chunks, and the cited chunks can be opened with page numbers.
- On the one paper both models completed, they gave the same answer on all 5 questions both answered
  (the two abstained on different cells).

## Problems found

- Free-tier Gemini ran out of quota after about 8 answers (and a few earlier test calls); a pilot of more than one
  paper needs a paid or higher-quota key.
- gpt-oss-120b took a median 32 s per answer, far slower than Gemini (7.7 s). Not investigated; it may be reasoning effort or
  free-tier throttling.
- The runner behaved as intended: the two failed Gemini jobs were reported and did not stop the run.

## Not covered

Accuracy against humans (no consensus file), the Claude and OpenAI models (no keys), Mistral small/medium (rate limited), and
runs on more than 3 papers.
